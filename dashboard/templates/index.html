How does grading work?

Your network starts with 100 points. Each alert from the last 24 hours takes some away:

  • Act now: 25 points
  • Worth checking: 10 points
  • Just so you know: 3 points

The points come back when you press “Got it” on an alert, or once it's more than a day old. If you change your mind, find it on the History page and choose “Not fixed yet”.
