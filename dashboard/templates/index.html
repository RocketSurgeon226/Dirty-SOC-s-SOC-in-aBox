How does grading work?

Your network starts with 100 points. Each open problem from the last 7 days takes a share of the points you have left, once per device, however many times it happened:

  • Act now: a quarter (100 becomes 75)
  • Worth checking: a tenth (100 becomes 90)
  • Just so you know: a little (100 becomes 97)

Each kind of problem counts at most twice, so one noisy device can't sink the grade. Each card says how many points fixing it adds back.

The points come back when you press “Got it” or “This is normal for this device”, or after a quiet week. If the same problem happens again after “Got it”, it comes back as a new card.
